## Semantic and formal review

The review rechecks the preceding construct assessment after replacing its proof
premises with explicit normative methods. Comparisons cover current decision
bodies, all-ancestor constraints, initializer precedence, nominal origins,
capabilities, private diagnostics, numeric signatures/rounding, nested shape,
functional branch demand, quantifier witness demand and body/recovery permissions.
Every concrete construct chapter retains its early short syntax example.

The certification review examines conservative target transfer, least fixed
points, typed policy/operator origins, finite vocabularies, nonexpansive generic
closure, availability pruning, mixed affine elimination, integral congruence
periods, natural tuples, declaration phases, exact affine implication and binary64
rounding. Unsupported source relations remain opaque. Finite model elimination
is not a sampled execution or a complete nonlinear solver.

Counterexamples include apparent versus real call cycles, unknown targets,
wildcard overlap, natural-counter reset, mutual same-argument calls, affine
counter transfer, rational halving, absorbed Rum decrement, subnormal descent
and transient private cardinality. Declared domain/cardinality bounds are proof
premises only when established in the actual view; a later checkpoint is not an
earlier private-prefix invariant. Constant policy code origin never establishes
functional value equality. Independent action/test/domain/local/schema cycle
bans remain mandatory.

Formal checks cover defined domains and variables, exact versus abstract models,
strict/equal lexicographic relations, finite profile/phase search, rational
coefficient scaling, full recursive-edge coverage, total measure definedness,
normal versus faulting regions and explicit completion. Body completion includes
Fault or permitted Refusal and does not promise successful evaluation. The nominal
HIR was reviewed under MUD-EDIT-004: no context, rank, policy identity or typed
effect fields were added.
