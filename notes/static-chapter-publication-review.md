# Static chapter publication review

## Scope and authority

Review date: 2026-10-14. This review covers chapters 12–26, supporting 01–02
and their developed nominal, typing, expression and effect dependencies.
It records publication evidence, not language rules. Descriptor/dependency
chapter 06, pending interoperability 27, causal algorithms 29–34, patches and
host protocols remain excluded. Unrelated causal-author work is preserved.

retain dynamic-call impact and version-uniform proof/certification procedures.

## Publication checks

Semantic review compares the construct contracts with current decisions and
canonical chapters, including initialization precedence, diamond origins,
strict membership, family associated-data completeness, value equality,
visibility and immutable capabilities. Counterexamples include cycles, incomplete
initializers, illegal member mutation, ambiguous contexts, unsafe public type
frontiers and schema-access laundering through private diagnostics.

Formal review checks the direction/closure of ancestry, independently defined
identity versus generation, finite origin sets, family application/member
identity, initialization and dependency judgements, order versus enumeration
and explicit proof premises. No implementation representation is prescribed.

Editorial review checks status/authority, current-state-only normative bodies,
traceability in metadata, active versus closed questions, early construction
examples and developed wiki targets. Chapter 10 and nominal HIR were reviewed
under MUD-EDIT-004; typed certificates and diagnostics create no nominal fields.

Mechanical review covers grammar reachability/uniqueness, CST/AST coverage,
static type/effect evidence, editorial/question/decision gates, export-profile
coverage and the complete temporary inventory. The current suites pass 72 syntax,
82 type, 13 effect and 3 export-profile tests (170 total). Certificates validate
supplied finite contracts; they are not a compiler, runtime or general solver.

Each major review has a fresh outside-repository Markdown inconsistency list.
The fourth final repository-wide review recorded an explicitly empty list.
All 32 task review/helper temporaries were removed before promotion. Chapters
02/12/14 completed proposed → in-review → current; the local publication commit
records only these eligible chapters. No push or history rewriting is authorised.
