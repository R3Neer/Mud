# ADR-167 — Certified pure recursion and ranking search

## Accepted choices

The author accepts finite lexicographic tuples of natural measures, constant
phases per declaration/code origin, certified recursion in Boolean rules,
look/sublook and alias operator bodies as well as functional dictionaries, and
finite Rum order ranking on actual rounded outputs. Independent action/test,
domain and local/schema cycle prohibitions remain. None grants source all Rum
or numeric Rum progression.

The author also accepts automatic scalar affine synthesis with natural
coefficients by an exact common linear procedure. It supplements elementary and
written measures rather than requiring a source annotation/helper expression.
It is not arbitrary nonlinear ranking synthesis or trial coefficient search.

## Formalization

MUD-TYPE-044 and specification/types/termination-certification.md define eligible
classes, a finite elementary catalogue/profile search, phase compression and
exact affine feasibility/verification. Different contexts of the same original
applied declaration/code origin share phase constants. Natural well-foundedness
and strict continuing-edge obligations prove body completion conditionally on
the independent admitted subcomputations. Static type/normal-result checking
retains its existing unique inference/annotation rules.

Composite functional applications accept the ordinary one-input tuple sugar;
structural aliases permit positional construction. Named/positional anonymous
products remain distinct. Functional code provenance creates no value equality,
public anchor or first-class callable category.

## Evidence and limits

Finite supplied certificates cover counter reset, mutual phases, affine descent,
exact rational halving failure, binary64 rounding stalls and subnormal descent.
Q-029 closes with the common procedure formalized by D-168. Resolution/wave,
exterior wait and deeper state-profile termination remain their existing Q-020,
Q-072 and Q-030 scopes; no compiler/runtime implementation is included.
