# Q-009 — Canonical IR form

## Content

What is the minimum versioned schema, how does it preserve provenance, and which normalisations does it perform?

Status: **partially decided** by [[notas/decisiones/ADR-051-grafo-semantico-e-ir-reconstruibles|D-051]].

The IR is reconstructible and versioned, uses anchors, preserves provenance and represents every semantic distinction listed in D-051. The executable schema, field names and compatibility rules remain to be defined.
